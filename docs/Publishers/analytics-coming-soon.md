Traditional analytics tools were built for human visitors. Most legacy tools track sessions, pageviews, and referrals — and they filter out bot traffic by design. That was fine when bots were noise. It's a problem now that they're some of the most active visitors on your site.

<br />TollBit Analytics fills that gap. Because it runs on server-side logs from your CDN or edge layer, it captures traffic that client-side tools miss entirely — including every AI crawler that hits your site without ever executing JavaScript. The result is a complete, accurate picture of your AI audience alongside your human one.

<br />From the dashboard you can see which agents are crawling your content and how often, which pages attract the most bot traffic, how frequently bots are bypassing your robots.txt directives, and the referral ratio between AI scrapes and actual human clickthroughs — the metric that reveals the real value exchange (or lack of one) happening between your site and AI platforms.

<br />This data is the foundation for everything else in TollBit. Understanding your bot traffic is what lets you make informed decisions about access controls, pricing, and monetization — whether you're looking to manage costs, protect content, or start generating revenue from AI access.
