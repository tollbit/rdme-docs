With your property verified, analytics enabled, and Agent Site configured, you're ready to start getting value from TollBit.  Here's where to go from here:

# Explore your analytics dashboard

Head to the analytics dashboard to see which AI agents are crawling your site, how often, and which pages are getting the most bot traffic. As data accumulates, you'll get a clearer picture of your AI audience and how they compare to your human visitors.

# Onboard to the Marketplace

Once you have a sense of your bot traffic, you can set a price on your content and establish license agreements for AI companies that want access. This is how you start monetizing the traffic that was previously hitting your site for free.

# Set up content controls

Fine-tune which agents can access your site, what content they can reach, and under what terms. You can allow, block, or charge different agents differently — giving you granular control over how your content is used in the AI era.

# Explore agentic protocols

TollBit supports MCP, NLWeb, and Agent2Agent (A2A) protocols, making your content accessible to a wider range of AI systems in standardized ways. See Other Access Methods → to enable these.

# Invite your team

If you haven't already, bring in the right stakeholders — legal for license agreements, finance for payout setup, and editorial or product for content control decisions.
